Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

YT App is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the .NET From Zero lesson videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

YT App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it uses

YT App requests only the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl permissions, which let it upload videos to the signed-in channel, add them to the channel's playlists and post a comment on them. It does not read, collect, or store any other data from your Google or YouTube account, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload videos, organise them into playlists and comment on them. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent anywhere other than Google's YouTube API.

Revoking access

You can revoke YT App's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the stored token also stops all uploads.
